# Tell members when a reservation is ready

When a copy comes back and a member has a hold on its title, the desk keeps the copy for them. Today the member only learns it when they next visit.

Send a notice the same day: the title, the branch and the last day the copy is kept. A patron who does not collect it within a week loses the hold, and the copy goes to the next member in the queue.
